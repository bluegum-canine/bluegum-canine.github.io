The qualification

960 hours,
34 modules

Dog training is unregulated in Ireland. Anyone may call themselves a trainer or a behaviourist, and most of what you will read on the subject is somebody's opinion with a number of years attached to it. So here is exactly what I was taught, where, and for how long.

Master Trainer Program, Highland Canine School for Dog Trainers, Harmony, North Carolina. Six months residential, living on campus, working dogs every day. Certificate dated December 2026.

412 hours in the classroom. 548 hours with dogs. The lab hours are the larger half deliberately — you cannot learn this from a lecture, and you cannot learn it properly from a weekend either.

Who taught it

Jason Purgason

The primary instructor at the School for Dog Trainers, President of Highland Canine Training, and current President of the International Association of Canine Professionals.

Thirty years working with dogs. A former police officer and police K9 handler who has run handler and instructor courses for law enforcement and military organisations, and trained more than a thousand dog teams in the United States and abroad.

I mention him for a specific reason. When you engage a trainer you are buying their judgement, and judgement is inherited — it comes from whoever taught them and how closely. Six months on campus is not a weekend seminar and it is not a correspondence course. It is being in the same field as your instructor, most days, being corrected.

That is the part I would want to know about, if I were hiring. Not that a certificate exists, but who stood over the work while it was being learned.

    Where behaviour is a medical problem wearing a behavioural costume. This is why health comes before behaviour, every time.

Alongside it

The other credential

I have been a full member of the International Association of Canine Professionals since 2025, which binds me to their code of conduct and to continuing professional development.

Of the trainers working in this part of Ireland, only a handful name any certifying body at all. It is worth asking anyone you are considering — not because a badge makes somebody good, but because an unwillingness to name one tells you something.
