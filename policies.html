Policies

The small print,
in plain English

Everything you might want to know before booking — who I work with, how payment works, and what happens if plans change. If anything here is unclear, ring me.

Who I work with

  • Any dog, of any breed, size or history
  • Puppies from eight weeks, at home. Until their vaccinations are finished, puppies are trained at home only, never in public places
  • Residential training from six months of age
  • Group classes for dogs that can cope near other dogs, once vaccinations are finished. A dog that isn’t ready yet starts one-to-one
  • Older dogs are welcome — I will be honest at the assessment about what is realistic
  • Females in season can carry on with lessons at home, but cannot start residential training or come to a group class until the season is over

Health and legal requirements

Every dog must be microchipped and registered to its current owner, licensed with the local council, and free of fleas, worms and anything contagious. Core vaccinations must be in date, and for residential training, kennel cough within the last twelve months.

I will ask to see the vaccination record and licence at the first visit. If your dog is on medication, has an injury, or has a medical history that could affect training, tell me before we start — it changes what I do.

Behaviour cases and dogs that bite

Tell me everything, including every bite, however small. I would rather know on day one than find out in session three. I will sometimes ask for a muzzle to be introduced before we work together, and I will show you how. I do not take a case I cannot handle safely — if that is the case I will say so, and refer you on.

Booking and payment

  • Single sessions and behaviour consultations — on the day, or before
  • Six-session package — in full at the first lesson
  • Group course — in full when you book your place
  • Residential training — half to book the place, half on collection
  • Residential for a serious behaviour problem — the €300 assessment comes first, and is credited against the fee if you go ahead

I accept bank transfer, Revolut and cheque. I do not take card payments. My bank details are on your invoice, and I will never send them any other way or ask you to pay into a different account by email or message. If anything ever looks different, ring me before you pay.

Cheques must have cleared before the session they pay for, and before a residential dog goes home. Allow five working days.

Prices are on the services page. Beyond seventy-five minutes’ drive there is a €60 supplement per visit, which I will tell you before I set off.

Cancelling or moving a session

  • Give me 24 hours’ notice and I will move the session at no cost
  • With less notice, or if you are not there when I arrive, the session is charged — or taken from your package
  • Genuine emergencies — your dog is ill, someone is in hospital — are never charged. Just ring me
  • If I have to cancel, you choose between the next available slot and a full refund for that session

Group courses

  • More than 14 days before the first class — refunded in full
  • Within 14 days of the first class — the fee is kept, unless I can fill the place
  • A class you miss is not refunded, but I will tell you what we covered so your dog doesn’t fall behind
  • If I cancel a class, it is added to the end of the course. If a course doesn’t run at all, you are refunded in full

Cancelling a residential place

  • More than 14 days before the start date — deposit refunded in full
  • Within 14 days of the start date — the deposit is kept, unless I can fill the place
  • The off-lead stage is booked, and paid for, separately from the on-lead stage
  • Collecting early, at your choice — the weeks already done are charged in full, and anything paid for weeks not started is refunded

While your dog is staying with me

  • Send enough of your dog’s usual food for the whole stay. If it runs out I will buy more of the same and add the cost to your bill
  • Please don’t send beds, blankets or soft toys. Dogs in a strange place chew things they would never touch at home, and swallowed fabric is a vet emergency
  • Have your dog groomed before it comes, if it needs it. I don’t arrange grooming during a stay
  • You are welcome to see where your dog will stay before you book. Once training has started, visits set it back by days, so you get updates and video twice a week instead. If you do want to visit, it is by arrangement, never unannounced
  • I check every dog every day. If anything looks wrong I ring you straight away rather than wait and see. What happens if I cannot reach you, and who pays the vet, is in the training agreement you sign before the stay
  • Your dog is collected on the agreed date, with the balance paid. The training agreement also sets out what happens if it is not

Refunds

There is no blanket “no refunds” rule. Unused package sessions are refunded: the sessions you have had are counted at the single-session price of €150, and the rest is returned. Refunds are made within 30 days, by bank transfer.

Your 14-day right to cancel

Because you book with me by phone, by message or at your own home, you have 14 days to change your mind and cancel without giving a reason. If you want training to start inside those 14 days, you will need to ask me to in writing — and if you then cancel, you pay only for the sessions already delivered. None of this affects your statutory rights.

What I guarantee — and what I don’t

I guarantee the work, not the dog. I cannot promise any particular result, because the outcome depends on the dog, on your household, and on what happens between my visits. Anyone who guarantees you a result in behaviour work is guessing.

What I do promise:

  • An honest assessment before you spend anything significant
  • A written plan, so you know exactly what we are doing and why
  • After residential training, two free follow-up sessions within six months, at your home, for whatever your dog tests you on once it is back in its own world
  • If a problem we trained for comes back and the follow-ups have not fixed it, I will work out with you what it takes — and tell you honestly if the answer is something other than more training

Follow-ups are for the problem we trained for. A tune-up before a holiday, or new training, is booked in the ordinary way.

Discounts

The six-session package is the discount: €765 instead of €900, fifteen per cent off. I do not run sales, voucher deals or introductory offers — the price reflects what the work takes, and everyone pays the same.

Photos and video

I film sessions because it is the best way to show you your dog’s progress. Nothing appears on my website or social media without your written consent, and you can withdraw it at any time. I never show your address, your house or your car registration.

Your information

I keep your contact details, your dog’s history and my session notes to do the work and to meet my insurance requirements. I do not sell or share them. I will only add you to a mailing list if you ask to be added. Ask at any time and I will tell you what I hold, or delete it.

Getting hold of me

Ring me — I answer the phone myself. If I cannot pick up, I will call you back, and if I say I will ring by a certain time and still do not have an answer, I will ring anyway to tell you so. If something has gone wrong, tell me directly first — most things are fixed with one conversation.
