Privacy

What I keep about you,
and why

A short version is on the policies page. This is the full version, as Irish and EU data protection law (the GDPR) requires. If anything here is unclear, ring me.

Who is responsible for your information

Mark Rabel, trading as Bluegum Canine (registered business name no. 792747, Companies Registration Office, Ireland). I work on my own, so the person you deal with is the person responsible. Contact me at mark@bluegumcanine.ie or on 085 738 6848.

What I collect

  • Your details — name, address and Eircode, phone number and email
  • Your dog’s details — history, health, vaccinations, medication, microchip and licence, pet insurance, and the vet you use
  • Behaviour history — including any bites, incidents or complaints, and who lives in the household, because I cannot work safely without it
  • My session notes and training plans
  • Photos and video of sessions, and any clips you send me
  • Payment records — what you paid, when and how. I never hold card details
  • Messages you send me by email, text or WhatsApp

Almost all of it comes from you. If you ask me to, I may also get information from your vet or from a trainer or behaviourist who referred you.

Why I use it, and on what basis

  • To do the training you booked — assessing your dog, planning the work, keeping in touch, taking payment. The legal basis is our contract
  • To meet legal obligations — tax and accounting records, and anything the law requires me to report
  • To protect both of us — keeping records my insurer requires, and being able to show what was done if there is ever a dispute or a claim. The legal basis is legitimate interests
  • With your consent — publishing photos or video of your dog, and adding you to a mailing list. You can withdraw consent at any time

I do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or make any automated decisions about you.

Photos and video

Your training agreement records what you have agreed to: published, published only without your face, or kept for the training record only. Nothing is published without that written consent. I never show your address, your house, your surname or your car registration. Change your mind at any time, in writing, and anything already published comes down within fourteen days.

Who I share it with

  • Your vet, if your dog needs treatment while in my care, or if you ask me to
  • My insurer, only if there is an incident or a claim
  • My accountant and Revenue, for tax records
  • Another trainer, behaviourist or vet I refer you to — only with your agreement
  • Anyone the law requires, such as a court or the Gardaí

Some ordinary services handle information on my behalf: my email provider, my bank, WhatsApp if you message me there, and GitHub, which hosts this website. Some of these are based outside the EU. Where they are, the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by standard contractual clauses.

How long I keep it

  • Clients — six years after our last session or programme, then deleted. That is how long Revenue requires business records to be kept, and how long a claim could be brought
  • Enquiries that do not become bookings — deleted within twelve months
  • Published photos and video — until you withdraw consent

This website

This site sets no cookies and does no tracking. There are no analytics, no advertising tags and no social media widgets, and the fonts are served from this site rather than from Google. There are no forms, so nothing you type is collected.

GitHub, which hosts the site, records visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes. I do not see or use those logs. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

If that ever changes, for example if I add visitor statistics, this page will say so before it happens.

Your rights

At any time you can ask me to:

  • tell you what I hold about you, and give you a copy
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete it, where I do not have to keep it by law
  • stop or limit using it, or object to how I use it
  • give it to you in a form you can pass to someone else

Ask by phone, email or in person. I will reply within one month, and there is no charge.

If you are not happy with how I have handled your information, tell me first. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission, Ireland’s data protection regulator.
