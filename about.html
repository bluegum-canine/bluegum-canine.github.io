About

Forty years,
then the paperwork

I have been around dogs for more than forty years. For most of that time it was simply what I did, without a certificate to point at.

In 2026 I put that right, and completed the Master Trainer Program at the Highland Canine School for Dog Trainers in North Carolina — 960 hours covering obedience, behaviour modification, aggression, scent and detection work, and the business of training itself. The certificate is dated December 2026.

It was worth doing for a reason I did not expect. Four decades of experience teaches you what works; the formal training teaches you why it works, and that turns out to matter enormously when a dog does not respond the way experience says it should. Knowing the mechanism is what lets you change the plan instead of repeating it louder.

I am a member of the International Association of Canine Professionals, and I hold to ethical, science-led best practice. In plain terms: I will tell you what I am doing and why, I will tell you honestly whether I can help, and I will not sell you a programme your dog does not need.
