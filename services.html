Services offered

How I can help

Six ways in. Which one suits you depends less on the problem than on how much of the work you want to do yourself.

Puppy foundations

Per session

The cheapest training you will ever buy, because it is the training that stops you needing the rest of this page. Socialisation done properly — which means controlled exposure, not simply meeting everything — handling, lead introduction, toilet training, and the household habits that decide what your dog is like at three.

  • Best started the week your puppy comes home
  • In your own house, where the habits actually form
  • Includes the arithmetic of what your puppy is learning between sessions

Everyday obedience

Per session

Recall, settle, loose lead, and stopping on command. The short list of things that decide whether a dog gets taken places or left at home. This is the freedom-through-obedience work, and for most families it is the whole job.

  • Taught, reinforced, proofed and maintained — in that order
  • Proofed around real distraction, not just in the garden
  • You are taught alongside the dog; otherwise it will not last

Behaviour consultation

Assessment + plan

Reactivity, aggression, anxiety and livestock worrying. This starts with a proper assessment: the dog’s history, what has been tried, and — most usefully — what has been paying off for the dog without anyone intending it. You get a written plan you can actually work.

  • Assessment where the behaviour happens, not in a training hall
  • Written plan and follow-up support while you work it
  • Health before behaviour: a sudden change with no obvious cause goes to your vet before it comes to me

One-to-one sessions

Per session or block

Private training at your home, or on ground of your choosing. Suited to dogs that cannot yet cope in a class, to owners who want to move faster than a group allows, and to problems that only ever happen in one particular place.

  • Single sessions or a block, whichever fits the problem
  • Sligo, Leitrim, Roscommon, Longford, Mayo and Cavan as standard — further by arrangement
  • Travel is inside the fee to about forty-five minutes out; beyond that it is quoted before I set off — how far I travel

Residential training

Per week

Board-and-train, for when a problem needs a proper run at it rather than an hour a week. Your dog stays with me and works daily, in the conditions the behaviour actually needs. Numbers are deliberately small — a handful of dogs at a time, never a kennel block.

  • Daily work, structured to the four steps
  • Handover sessions at the end — the transfer to you is part of the job, not an extra
  • Progress updates while your dog is with me

Assistance dog work

By assessment

Task training for mobility, autism, signal work for deaf handlers, and dogs for seizures and PTSD. This is the largest single block of the Master Trainer programme — 334 hours — and it is the least served part of the market in Ireland. Work starts with an assessment of the handler's needs and the candidate dog, because not every dog can do this and it is kinder to find that out early.

The access side matters as much as the training. Ireland and the UK have no register, no certificate and no legal category called a service dog, which surprises most people — and accreditation, though not required by law, is what decides whether you can fly with the dog. What the law actually says about assistance dogs →

  • Handler and candidate dog assessed before anything is promised
  • Taken from anywhere in Ireland, not just the north west
  • A straight answer about accreditation and public access at the start, rather than at an airport

If I am not the right person for your dog, I will say so and tell you who is.
