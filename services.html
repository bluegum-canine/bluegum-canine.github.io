Services offered

How I can help

Prices are on this page because you should not have to ring a stranger to find out whether you can afford them. Payment, cancellations and refunds are on the policies page.

Six ways in. Which one suits you depends less on the problem than on how much of the work you want to do yourself.

Puppy foundations

€150 a session

The cheapest training you will ever buy, because it is the training that stops you needing the rest of this page. Socialisation done properly — which means controlled exposure, not simply meeting everything — handling, lead introduction, toilet training, and the household habits that decide what your dog is like at three.

  • Best started the week your puppy comes home
  • In your own house, where the habits actually form
  • Includes the arithmetic of what your puppy is learning between sessions

Everyday obedience

€150 a session

Recall, settle, loose lead, and stopping on command. The short list of things that decide whether a dog gets taken places or left at home. This is the freedom-through-obedience work, and for most families it is the whole job.

  • Taught, reinforced, proofed and maintained — in that order
  • Proofed around real distraction, not just in the garden
  • You are taught alongside the dog; otherwise it will not last

Group classes

€175 for six weeks

Everyday obedience in a small group: recall, settle, loose lead and stopping on command, taught alongside other dogs and their people. Other dogs are the distraction the work has to stand up to, which is what makes a class worth doing, and cheaper than private lessons.

  • Six weekly classes of about an hour. Dates, times and venues to be confirmed. Ring or email to be told first
  • Kept small, so every dog gets worked, not just watched
  • For dogs that can cope near other dogs. If yours can’t yet, that isn’t a no — we start one-to-one and join a class once it is ready
  • Vaccinations finished, and not in season

Behaviour consultation

€350 assessment + plan

Reactivity, aggression, anxiety and livestock worrying. This starts with a proper assessment: the dog’s history, what has been tried, and — most usefully — what has been paying off for the dog without anyone intending it. You get a written plan you can actually work.

  • Assessment where the behaviour happens, not in a training hall
  • Written plan and follow-up support while you work it
  • Health before behaviour: a sudden change with no obvious cause goes to your vet before it comes to me

One-to-one sessions

€150, or €765 for six

Private training at your home, or on ground of your choosing. Suited to dogs that cannot yet cope in a class, to owners who want to move faster than a group allows, and to problems that only ever happen in one particular place.

  • Single sessions at €150, or a block of six for €765 — fifteen per cent off — most behaviour work is a block
  • Sligo, Leitrim, Roscommon, Longford, Mayo and Cavan as standard — further by arrangement
  • Travel is inside the fee to about forty-five minutes out. Beyond seventy-five minutes there is a €60 supplement per visit, quoted before I set off — how far I travel

Residential training

From €2,100

Board-and-train, for when a problem needs a proper run at it rather than an hour a week. Your dog stays with me and works daily, in the conditions the behaviour actually needs. Numbers are deliberately small — a handful of dogs at a time, never a kennel block.

It is built in two stages, because a dog has to be reliable on the lead before it can be trusted off it. A fortnight is not long enough to send a dog home changed, so there is no shorter option.

  • Three weeks on lead — €2,100. Obedience: recall, settle, loose lead, stopping on command
  • Three weeks off lead — €2,100. The same obedience, taken off the lead. Only after the on-lead stage — and it can be booked months later, once the first stage has bedded in at home
  • Six weeks, both stages together — €4,200. On lead and off lead in one stay, and it includes work on a minor behaviour problem
  • More serious behaviour problems — aggression, guarding, livestock chasing — are taken on the six-week programme, with a supplement that depends on the problem. It starts with a €300 assessment, which comes off the fee if we go ahead, and you have the full price in writing before you book
  • Any training equipment I use with your dog is yours to keep, included in the price — and I show you how to use it at handover
  • Handover lesson at the end — the transfer to you is part of the job, not an extra — and two free follow-up sessions at home within six months
  • Progress updates twice a week while your dog is with me

If I am not the right person for your dog, I will say so and tell you who is.

Planned, not yet offered

Assistance dog work

From 2028, if there is demand

Task training for mobility, autism, signal work for deaf handlers, and dogs for seizures and PTSD. I am qualified to train service dogs, and it is the least served part of the market in Ireland, but it is not a service I am offering now. An assistance dog is a multi-year commitment for the family as much as the dog, and it deserves to be run properly or not at all. I may add it from 2028, depending on demand.

In the meantime I am happy to guide you and answer questions, whether you are thinking about an assistance dog, choosing a candidate dog, or working out where you stand on access. Ireland and the UK have no register, no certificate and no legal category called a service dog, which surprises most people. What the law actually says about assistance dogs →

  • Questions welcome from anywhere in Ireland. Ring or email
  • A straight answer about accreditation and public access, rather than finding out at an airport
  • If you would like to hear if and when the service opens, tell me and I will let you know
