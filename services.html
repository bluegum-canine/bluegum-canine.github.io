How I can help

Prices are on this page because you should not have to ring a stranger to find out whether you can afford them. Payment, cancellations and refunds are on the policies page.

Six ways in, each with its own page. Which one suits you depends less on the problem than on how much of the work you want to do yourself.

  1. 01

    Puppy foundations

    €150 a session. Training that starts the week your puppy comes home, in your own house.

  2. 02

    Everyday obedience

    €150 a session. Recall, settle, loose lead and stopping on command.

  3. 03

    Group classes

    €175 for six weeks. Everyday obedience in a small group, over six weeks.

  4. 04

    Behaviour consultation

    €350 assessment + plan. A proper assessment where the behaviour happens, and a written plan.

  5. 05

    One-to-one sessions

    €150, or €765 for six. Private training at your home, or on ground of your choosing.

  6. 06

    Residential training

    From €2,100. Board-and-train: your dog stays with me and works daily.

How it works

Three steps
to getting started

  1. 01

    We talk on the phone first

    Free, and no obligation. I would rather understand the problem before I arrive than form a view on the doorstep.

  2. 02

    I assess your dog where the behaviour happens

    A behaviour that only appears at the gate cannot be assessed in the kitchen. The visit is arranged and quoted on the call, so you know the cost before I travel.

  3. 03

    You get a written plan, and support while you work it

    Nobody remembers a training session three weeks later. Written down, it survives.
