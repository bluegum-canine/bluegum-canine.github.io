Services offered

How I can help

Prices are on this page because you should not have to ring a stranger to find out whether you can afford them. Payment, cancellations and refunds are on the policies page.

Five ways in, each with its own page. Which one suits you depends less on the problem than on how much of the work you want to do yourself.

Everyone starts the same way: an assessment visit at your home, €150. An hour or so, the dog in its own surroundings, and an honest recommendation at the end. It is deducted from whatever follows, group classes aside — and it is the same price as a one-to-one session, because that is essentially what it is.

  1. 01

    Puppy foundations

    €400 for three home visits. Training that starts the week your puppy comes home, in your own house.

  2. 02

    One-to-one training

    €150, or €765 for six. Everyday obedience at your home, or training towards one particular goal: the crate, the car, a retrieve, scent work. Allow up to ninety minutes a session.

  3. 03

    Group classes

    €175 for the whole six-week course, not per class. Everyday obedience in a small group.

  4. 04

    Behaviour consultation

    €350 all in, including the assessment visit, a written plan and a follow-up visit. For aggression, reactivity, fear and livestock chasing, assessed where the behaviour happens.

  5. 05

    Residential training

    From €2,100. Board-and-train: your dog stays with me and works daily.

Places are limited, and that is deliberate. I work with a small number of dogs at any one time so that each gets proper attention. If I am full when you get in touch, I will tell you how long the wait is and you can go on the list.

If I am not the right person for your dog, I will say so and tell you who is.

  2. –

How it works

Four steps
to getting started

  1. 01

    We talk first, and it costs nothing

    A phone call, no obligation. I ask a great many questions and you get a plain answer about whether this is something I can help with. If it is not, I will say so and point you at who can — I am not going to drive across three counties to tell you that on your doorstep. A video helps, if you can manage one. Footage of the dog actually doing it tells me more than any description can.

  2. 02

    The assessment visit — €150

    If it sounds like something I can take on, I come to you. Sixty to ninety minutes, sometimes less, sometimes longer if the dog needs it. I see the dog at home and in the places the behaviour actually happens, because a dog in its own kitchen shows you things a dog in a training hall never will. At the end you get a straight recommendation: one-to-one sessions, a group class, a behaviour programme, or residential training.

  3. 03

    That €150 comes off whatever we do next

    If you go ahead, the cost of the visit is deducted from it. The assessment is not an extra fee bolted on the front — it is the first €150 of the work. The one exception is group classes: at €175 for a six-week course there is not enough in it to discount, and I would rather say so plainly than bury it.

  4. 04

    You get a written plan, and support while you work it

    Nobody remembers a training session three weeks later. Written down, it survives.
