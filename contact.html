What to have ready

  1. 01

    Your dog’s age, breed and history

    Including where you got them and at what age, if you know.

  2. 02

    What actually happens

    Not what you think it means — just what the dog does, where, and when it started.

  3. 03

    What you have already tried

    This is genuinely useful and never held against you. It narrows things down quickly.

  4. 04

    A short video, if you can get one safely

    Only if it is safe to film. Never provoke the behaviour to get footage of it.
