Residential training

Board-and-train: your dog stays here with us, in small numbers, and works every day. Then I teach you to keep it going at home.

Puppy training Obedience Group classes Behaviour consultation One-to-one Residential

What it is

From €2,100

Board-and-train, for when a problem needs a proper run at it rather than an hour a week. Your dog stays with me and works daily, in the conditions the behaviour actually needs. Numbers are deliberately small — a handful of dogs at a time, never a kennel block.

It is built in two stages, because a dog has to be reliable on the lead before it can be trusted off it. A fortnight is not long enough to send a dog home changed, so there is no shorter option.

  • Three weeks on lead — €2,100. Obedience: recall, settle, loose lead, stopping on command
  • Three weeks off lead — €2,100. The same obedience, taken off the lead. Only after the on-lead stage — and it can be booked months later, once the first stage has bedded in at home
  • Six weeks, both stages together — €4,200. On lead and off lead in one stay, and it includes work on a minor behaviour problem
  • More serious behaviour problems — aggression, guarding, livestock chasing — are taken on the six-week programme, with a supplement that depends on the problem. It starts with a €300 assessment, which comes off the fee if we go ahead, and you have the full price in writing before you book
  • Any training equipment I use with your dog is yours to keep, included in the price — and I show you how to use it at handover
  • Handover lesson at the end — the transfer to you is part of the job, not an extra — and two free follow-up sessions at home within six months
  • Progress updates twice a week while your dog is with me

The terms

While your dog is staying with me

  • Send enough of your dog’s usual food for the whole stay. If it runs out I will buy more of the same and add the cost to your bill
  • Please don’t send beds, blankets or soft toys. Dogs in a strange place chew things they would never touch at home, and swallowed fabric is a vet emergency
  • Have your dog groomed before it comes, if it needs it. I don’t arrange grooming during a stay
  • You are welcome to see where your dog will stay before you book. Once training has started, visits set it back by days, so you get updates and video twice a week instead. If you do want to visit, it is by arrangement, never unannounced
  • I check every dog every day. If anything looks wrong I ring you straight away rather than wait and see. What happens if I cannot reach you, and who pays the vet, is in the training agreement you sign before the stay
  • Your dog is collected on the agreed date, with the balance paid. The training agreement also sets out what happens if it is not

Cancelling a residential place

  • More than 14 days before the start date — deposit refunded in full
  • Within 14 days of the start date — the deposit is kept, unless I can fill the place
  • The off-lead stage is booked, and paid for, separately from the on-lead stage
  • Collecting early, at your choice — the weeks already done are charged in full, and anything paid for weeks not started is refunded

All the terms are on the policies page.
