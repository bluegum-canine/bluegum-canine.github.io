Behaviour consultation

For a dog that is starting to worry you — reacting, frightened, guarding, or chasing stock. A proper assessment where the behaviour happens, and a written plan you can actually work.

Puppy training Obedience Group classes Behaviour consultation One-to-one Residential

What it is

€350 assessment + plan

Reactivity, aggression, anxiety and livestock worrying. This starts with a proper assessment: the dog’s history, what has been tried, and — most usefully — what has been paying off for the dog without anyone intending it. You get a written plan you can actually work.

  • Assessment where the behaviour happens, not in a training hall
  • Written plan and follow-up support while you work it
  • Health before behaviour: a sudden change with no obvious cause goes to your vet before it comes to me

The terms

Behaviour cases and dogs that bite

Tell me everything, including every bite, however small. I would rather know on day one than find out in session three. I will sometimes ask for a muzzle to be introduced before we work together, and I will show you how. I do not take a case I cannot handle safely — if that is the case I will say so, and refer you on.

All the terms are on the policies page.

Related: barking and lunging at other dogs, guarding food and toys, nervous around people, livestock worrying, and the case studies.

If I am not the right person for your dog, I will say so and tell you who is.
