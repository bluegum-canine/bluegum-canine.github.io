One-to-one sessions

Private training at your home, or on ground of your choosing — for dogs that are not ready for a class, and problems that only happen in one place.

What it is

€150, or €765 for six

Private training at your home, or on ground of your choosing. Suited to dogs that cannot yet cope in a class, to owners who want to move faster than a group allows, and to problems that only ever happen in one particular place.

  • Single sessions at €150, or a block of six for €765 — fifteen per cent off — most behaviour work is a block
  • Sligo, Leitrim, Roscommon, Longford, Mayo and Cavan as standard — further by arrangement
  • Travel is inside the fee to about forty-five minutes out. Beyond seventy-five minutes there is a €60 supplement per visit, quoted before I set off — how far I travel

Scent work, as an add-on

Part of a session

Not a separate service, and not an extra charge — searching is something we can put into your sessions, or spend a whole one on if you enjoy it. Boxes, containers, a room, the car. Your dog learns to find one particular smell and tell you where it is.

  • A dog works on its own, so a dog that cannot yet cope near other dogs can do it from day one — and be good at something, which matters more than it sounds
  • It tires a dog out mentally in a way a walk does not, which helps the dogs that never seem to switch off
  • It suits older, arthritic and recovering dogs, and dogs on restricted exercise
  • Started in your own house, with nothing you need to buy

A group block is planned once there is a hall for the winter. Ask to be told when, or read how a dog learns a smell.

How a block of sessions runs

Built around your dog,
not a syllabus

There is no fixed curriculum. Before the first session I want to know how your dog actually lives — where it sleeps, who feeds it, what a normal day looks like, what has been tried and what happened. That is where most answers are hiding.

The first session goes straight at the thing that worries you most. You should see something change on the first day, even if it is small, so you know the plan is worth working. The first session usually runs longer than the rest.

After that, sessions are about an hour, usually a week apart. Each one starts with how the week went, because what happens between sessions matters far more than what happens during them. Then we move the work on a step: a busier place, a longer wait, a closer distraction.

What I will ask of you

An hour a week with me is not going to beat the rest of the week on its own. Your dog is learning all day, from everything, so the work is mostly yours — and it is mostly small.

  • House rules, written down. What the dog is and isn’t allowed to do, agreed by everyone and stuck on the fridge. It sounds trivial. It is usually the first thing that changes the dog
  • The same words from everyone. If one person says “down” for lie down and another says it for get off the sofa, the dog is not being disobedient
  • A few minutes, several times a day, rather than one long session at the weekend
  • Honesty about the week. If the homework didn’t happen, tell me, and we will make it smaller rather than pretend

Who one-to-one suits

  • Dogs that cannot yet cope near other dogs, so a class isn’t fair on them or on you
  • Problems that only happen in one place: your gate, your car, your road, your visitors
  • Owners who want to move faster than a group allows, or who would simply rather not have an audience
  • Anything that is starting to frighten you — that usually begins with a behaviour consultation and carries on as a block of sessions

It is not a way to have the dog fixed without you. If you need someone else to do the heavy lifting first, residential training exists for exactly that, and you are still taught at the end of it.

Common questions

How many sessions will we need? I will tell you honestly once I have met your dog. Most behaviour work is a block of six; a single everyday problem can take fewer.

Where do we train? At home first, where the habits are formed, then wherever the problem actually happens.

Should the whole family be there? Whoever walks and feeds the dog, at least. A dog that listens to one person is only partly trained.

The terms

Cancelling or moving a session

  • Give me 24 hours’ notice and I will move the session at no cost
  • With less notice, or if you are not there when I arrive, the session is charged — or taken from your package
  • Genuine emergencies — your dog is ill, someone is in hospital — are never charged. Just ring me
  • If I have to cancel, you choose between the next available slot and a full refund for that session
