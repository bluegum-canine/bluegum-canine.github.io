What it is

€175 for six weeks

Everyday obedience in a small group: recall, settle, loose lead and stopping on command, taught alongside other dogs and their people. Other dogs are the distraction the work has to stand up to, which is what makes a class worth doing, and cheaper than private lessons.

  • Six weekly classes of about an hour. Dates, times and venues to be confirmed. Ring or email to be told first
  • Kept small, so every dog gets worked, not just watched
  • For dogs that can cope near other dogs. If yours can’t yet, that isn’t a no — we start one-to-one and join a class once it is ready
  • Vaccinations finished, and not in season
  • A scent work block is planned once there is a hall for the winter — dogs search one at a time, so it is open to dogs that cannot yet work near others. What scent work is
