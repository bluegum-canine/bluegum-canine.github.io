What it is

Planned from 2028

Task training for mobility, autism, signal work for deaf handlers, and dogs for seizures and PTSD. I am qualified to train service dogs, and it is the least served part of the market in Ireland, but it is not a service I am offering now. An assistance dog is a multi-year commitment for the family as much as the dog, and it deserves to be run properly or not at all. I plan to add it from 2028.

In the meantime I am happy to guide you and answer questions, whether you are thinking about an assistance dog, choosing a candidate dog, or working out where you stand on access. Ireland and the UK have no register, no certificate and no legal category called a service dog, which surprises most people. What the law actually says about assistance dogs →

  • Questions welcome from anywhere in Ireland. Ring or email
  • A straight answer about accreditation and public access, rather than finding out at an airport
  • If you would like to hear if and when the service opens, tell me and I will let you know

If I am not the right person for your dog, I will say so and tell you who is.
