Why I say ring early

Most dogs in the pound
were not lost

They were handed in. That is the part of the local figures that stopped me, and it is the reason this page exists at all.

Every council in Ireland reports what happens in its dog pound. In 2024, 134 dogs went into the pound in Co. Sligo. Only 42 of them arrived as strays.

77 dogs surrendered by their own owners — nearly twice the number that came in as strays. Nationally the ratio runs the other way, roughly three strays to every one surrender
8 rehomed directly from the pound. Most of the rest were passed on to rescue and welfare groups, who were already full
25 did not leave. They were put to sleep in the course of the year

Source: Local Authority Control of Dogs Statistics Report 2024, Department of Rural and Community Development

Set against the size of the county, that last figure is the second-highest rate of dog euthanasia per head of population in the State — close to three times the national average. Only Clare’s is higher, and only by one dog.

What I am not saying

That is not the pound’s failure, and it is not the rescues’ either. A pound can only place a dog that somebody is willing to take, and every rescue in the northwest is turning dogs away already.

Nor am I going to tell you all 77 were behaviour cases. The report does not record why dogs are given up, and I am not going to invent a figure to make a point. Marriages end, people fall ill, tenancies change, and dogs lose homes for reasons no trainer could have touched.

What I am saying

Some of them were behaviour. Anybody who has worked with dogs knows what the surrender conversations sound like — he growled at the baby, she has started going for other dogs, we cannot leave him, it has been getting worse for a year. That last part is the one that matters. It is almost never the first week.

Every problem on this page is easier at eight weeks than at eight months, and easier at eight months than after somebody has been bitten. Not because the dog becomes untrainable — most do not — but because the behaviour gets more practised, the household gets more worn down, and the room to work in gets smaller.

So: ring while it is still small and annoying. The first conversation costs nothing, and if it is something you can sort out yourself I will tell you that and you will have lost a phone call.
